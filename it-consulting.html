Enterprise Technology

Aligning Technology Investments With Business Realities

Technology should be an enabler of business velocity, not an unmanageable cost sink or source of operational inertia.

We provide vendor-neutral assessments of legacy systems, evaluate enterprise software options (ERP, CRM, HRM), review cloud hosting alternatives, and construct pragmatic IT modernization roadmaps.

Architecture Blueprint

Enterprise Systems Assessment Framework

Evaluating the layers of modern enterprise IT infrastructure.

Layer 1

Core Applications (ERP / CRM)

Evaluating feature fit, data normalization, total cost of ownership (TCO), and upgrade paths for transactional business software.

Layer 2

Integration & Data Pipes

Designing unified API architectures and data pipelines to prevent data silos between sales, finance, and operations.

Layer 3

Infrastructure & Cloud Hosting

Reviewing cloud migration feasibility, disaster recovery protocols, service level agreements (SLAs), and hosting budgets.

Software Selection Advisory

Structured RFP preparation, vendor demonstration scoring, and contractual negotiation guidance for major enterprise software purchases.

Cybersecurity Policy Planning

Reviewing data classification policies, access controls, backup regimens, and staff security hygiene protocols.

IT Project Oversight

Independent advisory oversight during high-stakes software rollouts to ensure vendor adherence to budget, timeline, and scope.
